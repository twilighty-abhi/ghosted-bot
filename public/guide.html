👻 GHOSTED Bot Guide

The definitive guide to managing your student cohorts and teams.

1 The Google Sheet (The Foundation)

Before using the bot, you must format your Google Sheet correctly so the bot understands who belongs to which team.

  • The sheet requires exactly three columns (the order doesn't matter): Name, Discord ID, and Team.
  • Click Share in the top right of your Google Sheet.
  • Set General access to "Anyone with the link" can "Viewer".
Finding Discord IDs: Students must enable "Developer Mode" in Discord settings to right-click their own profile and copy their 18-digit Discord ID.

2 Creating a New Cohort (Provisioning)

When a new cohort launches, you use the Provision tab to instantly build all their channels and roles.

  • Enter the Cohort Number (e.g., 2).
  • Paste the Google Sheet URL and click Preview.
  • Review the live preview grid. You will see exactly how many teams are being built.
  • If there are yellow spreadsheet warnings (e.g., invalid Discord IDs), open your spreadsheet and fix them! Hit preview again to refresh.
  • Decide if you want a Welcome Message posted, then click ⚡ Provision.

The bot will create the main category, announcement and general channels, private team channels, and assign the proper roles to everyone currently inside the Discord server.

3 Adding Late Joiners (Syncing)

If a student joins the Discord server after you've already provisioned the cohort, you need to sync them.

  • Ensure their Discord ID is updated in the Google Sheet.
  • In the Provision tab, enter the Cohort Number and Sheet URL, and click Preview.
  • The grid will show ⏳ next to students who are newly in the server but missing roles.
  • Click 🔄 Sync.

Sync strictly gives roles to missing members and ignores everyone else. It does not create new channels!

4 Manual Overrides (Members Tab)

Sometimes you need to quickly fix a single student without messing with spreadsheets. Head to the Members tab.

  • Add Single Member: Paste their Discord ID, select their Cohort, type their team tag, and hit Add.
  • Transfer Teams: Enter their ID, their old team, and their new team. The bot will instantly swap their roles.

5 Communication (Comms Tab)

Organizers can use the bot to broadcast messages without leaving the dashboard.

  • Broadcasts: Dumps your exact message directly into a cohort's locked #ghosted-announcements channel.
  • Team DMs: Select a specific Cohort and Team (e.g., Cohort 2, Team "dalai-lama"). Type your message, and the bot will physically Direct Message every student in that team.
Use Team DMs sparingly—Discord restricts bots that spam too many Direct Messages at once!

6 Tearing Down Cohorts (Manage Tab)

When a cohort naturally concludes, you can clean up the server to preserve space.

  • View Stats: Check live, up-to-date counts of how many real people exist in each team.
  • Export CSV: Downloads the exact roster of the active cohort for your records.
  • Bulk Remove: Instantly rips the `Ghosted-cohort` and `Ghosted-team` roles away from every student in the cohort. They lose access immediately.
  • Archive Cohort: (Available via slash-commands inside Discord) Locks all channels so students can read them but never type again.

Finally, you can check the Activity Log at the bottom of the Manage tab to see a permanent audit trail of who transferred who, and when cohorts were built!
